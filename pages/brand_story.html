N Seoul Tower

: A Romantic Island in the Heart of the City

Where the sky meets the clouds

N Seoul Tower is a multifunctional cultural space in Seoul that offers a perfect harmony of Namsan's natural beauty and 21st-century cutting-edge technology, along with leisurely relaxation and a diverse array of cultural experiences.

02

About

N Seoul Tower

: A Romantic Island in the Heart of the City

Located in the heart of Seoul

N Seoul Tower is a symbol of the city and the highest vantage point from which to take in Seoul's most beautiful views at a glance. It was built in 1969 as Korea's first comprehensive broadcast tower to transmit TV and radio signals to the Seoul metropolitan area.

03

About

N Seoul Tower

: A Romantic Island in the Heart of the City

48% of radio listeners nationwide receive broadcasts through N Seoul Tower.

This tower is equipped not only with transmission antennas for KBS, MBC, and SBS TV and FM, but also with antennas for PBC, TBS, CBS, and BBS FM.

About

N Seoul Tower

: A Romantic Island in the Heart of the City

Since opening to the public in 1980

N Seoul Tower has established itself as a place of relaxation for Seoul citizens and a tourist attraction for foreigners, harmonizing with the vibrant natural surroundings of Namsan Mountain.

About

N Seoul Tower

: A Romantic Island in the Heart of the City

A place that shines beautifully at any time

In an "Art of Light" environment where colors and patterns change moment by moment thanks to state-of-the-art LED lighting you can experience unique cultural and artistic expressions alongside diverse media art installations.

Reborn as Seoul's premier multifunctional cultural space, N Seoul Tower is set to become a proud landmark of Seoul.
